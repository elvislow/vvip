    About

    I design posters that people keep.

    Ten years of turning pencil sketches into printed work, first at a print shop, then a theatre, and since 2022 from my own studio in Lisbon.

    I learned print production as an artwork technician at Grafica Ribeira, preparing files for two offset lines and standing at a lot of press checks. Then I designed three seasons of posters for Teatro do Cais.

    Since 2022 my independent studio has worked with venues, roasters, shelters and record labels. Most of the work ends up on a wall: the street, a bar, a shop window, someone’s kitchen.

    Outside the studio I teach an evening course in screen printing, collect risograph ephemera and run slowly along the river.

      How I work

      • Every job starts with a written brief we both sign off, even if it is four lines long.
      • Three sketched directions first, then one idea pushed hard. No twelve-option decks.
      • Colour is approved on a printed proof on the real stock, never on a screen.
      • I attend the press check. If the run looks wrong, it stops.
      • Source files and a one-page usage note are always part of the handover.

      What I care about

      One idea per poster

      A poster has about two seconds across a street. It gets one image, one hierarchy and nothing that needs explaining.

      Made for the wall

      Stock, ink and finish shape the design from the first sketch, not after it.

      On time

      Every schedule includes a printed proof and a day of slack before the press.

      Colophon

      Schibsted Grotesk

      Geist Mono for numbers and specs

      Both typefaces are open-source and self-hosted. Photography of the studio and the posters in place was made for this site. Everything you see in colour is client work.
